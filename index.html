I'm Kalakalyani, a psychologist living in Thrissur, Kerala, with over thirty years of quiet practice. People come to me when they feel heavy, confused, or stuck, and they leave a little lighter.

Through one-on-one guidance and the classes and workshops I have run across Kerala, I have walked alongside students, families, and elders. My way is simple: sit quietly, listen kindly, and help each person find their own strength again.
