My Process

How I work

From the first message to the final launch — here's what working with me actually looks like.

01

Discovery

We start with a call or message to understand your business, goals, and target audience. No templates — every project starts from scratch with your needs in mind.

02

Design

I create high-fidelity Figma mockups for your review before writing a single line of code. You see exactly what you're getting and can request changes freely.

03

Build

Clean, semantic HTML, CSS & JavaScript — hand-coded for speed and accessibility. You get progress updates throughout so there are no surprises at the end.

04

Launch & Support

I handle deployment, final testing, and a 2-week post-launch support window so you're never left stranded. Ongoing retainers available for growing businesses.
