Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the therapeutic process and our practice.

1. What can I expect during my first session?

The first session is primarily an assessment where we discuss your concerns, history, and goals for therapy. It's a time for us to get to know each other and determine if we're a good fit for working together.

2. How long does a typical session last?

Most individual therapy sessions are 50 minutes long. Couples and family sessions may sometimes be longer, depending on the situation and prior arrangement.

3. Is therapy confidential?

Yes, confidentiality is a cornerstone of psychological practice. Information shared in sessions is strictly protected, with a few legal and ethical exceptions which we will discuss in our first session.

4. Do you offer online therapy sessions?

Absolutely. I offer secure and private video consultations for clients who prefer or require remote therapy, including those across India and abroad.

5. How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies greatly depending on the individual and the nature of their concerns. We will periodically review your progress and adjust the treatment plan as needed.

6. What are your fees and payment methods?

Fees for sessions are discussed during the initial consultation. We accept various payment methods, including bank transfers and major digital wallets in India.
