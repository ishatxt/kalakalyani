Biography & Experience

Dr. Kala Kalyani is a dedicated psychologist with a decade of experience helping clients through various life challenges. Her journey in the field of mental health began with a deep curiosity about the human mind and a desire to provide support to those in need.

For the past 10 years, she has practiced in diverse settings, from clinical hospitals to private practice, allowing her to develop a well-rounded and deeply empathetic approach to therapy.
